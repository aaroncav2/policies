Privacy Policy – Project Pathways

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Project Pathways ("the app") is a free educational board game for Android, developed by an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information I collect

I do not collect, store, or sell personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, and no servers of its own. Game progress and any study card files you import (CSV) stay on your device and are not sent to me.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use certain data from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This data is handled by Google under its own policies; I do not receive your personal data from it.

See how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app shows a consent form before personalized ads. You can change your choice through that form when it is offered.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in Settings → Privacy (or Google → Ads).
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data stored by the app on your device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect information from children.
